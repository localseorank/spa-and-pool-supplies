Comparing Suction, Pressure, and Robotic Automatic Pool Cleaners

When shopping for an automatic pool cleaner in Melbourne, understanding the three primary categories—suction-side cleaners, pressure-side cleaners, and autonomous robotic cleaners—helps you make an informed investment. Each system offers unique mechanical benefits suited to different pool configurations, plumbing setups, and maintenance budgets.

Suction-side cleaners are the most affordable and widely adopted automatic pool cleaners across Australian backyards. These units attach directly to your skimmer box or dedicated vacuum plate, utilizing the suction power of your main pool filtration pump to navigate the pool floor and walls. Modern disc and hammer suction cleaners from Spa & Pool Supplies are engineered with anti-stick deflectors and durable diaphragms, providing dependable cleaning for fine silt and light leaf loads at an accessible entry price.

Pressure-side pool cleaners operate via pressurized water supplied either by a secondary booster pump or your return line. These cleaners incorporate internal debris sweep bags, capturing large leaves, sticks, and heavy eucalyptus debris before it enters your skimmer box or sand filter. For Melbourne properties surrounded by dense deciduous trees or sprawling bushland, pressure-side cleaners deliver outstanding heavy-debris management without choking your skimmer basket.

Autonomous robotic cleaners represent the peak of modern convenience and energy efficiency. Powered by low-voltage internal electric motors and equipped with onboard filtration canisters, these systems operate completely independent of your pool's central pump. At Spa & Pool Supplies, our range of Robotic Pool Cleaners Melbourne families love actively scrubs floors, walls, and waterlines, slashing electricity consumption and eliminating backwash cycles.

How to Select the Best Affordable Automatic Cleaner for Your Pool Type

Matching your automatic pool cleaner to your swimming pool's specific engineering and landscaping is essential for achieving trouble-free, reliable results. An automatic cleaner that works wonders in a compact fibreglass plunge pool may struggle with the abrasive surface or complex step ledges of an expansive concrete pool.

When choosing an automatic pool cleaner from Spa & Pool Supplies, evaluate these critical factors:

  • ✓Pool Interior Surface: Smooth fibreglass and vinyl pools require cleaners with soft pleated seals or foam rollers to prevent slipping, while rough pebblecrete and quartzon need durable ribbed discs or combi-brushes.
  • ✓Filtration Pump Horsepower: If selecting a suction-side cleaner, verify that your pool pump has adequate wattage (minimum 0.75 HP to 1.5 HP) to generate the required water flow.
  • ✓Backyard Vegetation & Debris: Properties facing heavy eucalyptus leaves and twigs benefit from wide-throat cleaners or dedicated debris bags, whereas dust-prone environments require fine micro-filtration.
  • ✓Pool Shape and Steps: Pools with intricate benches, swim-up bars, or sharp 90-degree corners require cleaners equipped with bumper wheels and multi-directional steering deflectors.

Melbourne’s harsh climate extremes also demand durable build materials. High summer UV indices and cold winter dampness can quickly degrade low-grade plastics. Cleaners supplied by Spa & Pool Supplies are constructed from heavy-duty, UV-stabilized thermoplastics engineered specifically for Australian conditions.

Step-by-Step Installation and Routine Maintenance Guide for Automatic Cleaners

Installing and maintaining an automatic pool cleaner is straightforward, and following proper operational guidelines ensures smooth navigation and extended component lifespan. Spa & Pool Supplies recommends following these step-by-step instructions:

  1. 1Balance pool water and backwash filter: Ensure your pool water chemistry is balanced, empty skimmer and pump baskets, and perform a standard backwash or cartridge rinse to ensure maximum hydraulic flow.
  2. 2Assemble hose sections and purge air: Connect the flexible hose sections together, submerging the hose underwater section by section until all air is expelled and water flows freely from the connection end.
  3. 3Attach to skimmer vacuum plate: Insert the speed control valve into the skimmer vacuum plate, adjust flow rate to the manufacturer's recommended setting, and connect the vacuum hose securely.
  4. 4Deploy cleaner and check trajectory: Lower the cleaner head into the water, verify that it adheres firmly to the pool floor, observe its navigation pattern across the depths, and fine-tune flow control as needed.

Proper hose management is critical for avoiding memory coils and tangled sections. Periodically detach the hose sections and lay them straight along the lawn in the sun to eliminate kinks and retain flexibility. Replace cracked hose segments promptly to prevent air leaks that reduce suction pressure.

When removing your automatic pool cleaner for parties or winterizing, store the unit and hose sections in a shaded, covered space out of direct sunlight. Spa & Pool Supplies stocks replacement diaphragms, foot pads, and hose lengths to keep your automatic cleaner operating like new.
