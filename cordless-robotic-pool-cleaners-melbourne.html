Step-by-Step Guide to Operating and Recharging Your Cordless Cleaner

Operating a cordless robotic pool cleaner is remarkably straightforward, but observing manufacturer charging and deployment steps protects battery longevity and keeps your pool sparkling. Spa & Pool Supplies recommends establishing a consistent routine to safeguard your investment and enjoy hassle-free cleaning.

Follow this step-by-step operating process for optimal cordless cleaner performance:

  1. 1Fully charge the battery module: Connect the certified charging adapter to the dry robot charging port, allowing the battery to reach 100 percent capacity before disconnecting the charger and sealing the waterproof charging cap securely.
  2. 2Submerge and initiate cleaning cycle: Press the power button to activate your desired cleaning mode, lower the robot gently into the pool by its handle, and rock it slightly to expel trapped air until it sinks smoothly to the floor.
  3. 3Allow autonomous cleaning and self-parking: Let the cleaner complete its cycle across pool surfaces; upon completion, the robot will automatically park itself beside the pool wall for convenient retrieval.
  4. 4Retrieve with hook and rinse canister: Attach the included floating retrieval hook to your standard pool pole, hook the robot handle to bring it to the edge, lift it out, open the top canister, and thoroughly hose clean the filter panels before storage.

Proper battery charging hygiene is crucial for maximizing lithium-ion lifespan. Always ensure the charging port is completely dry before connecting the charging cable, and never leave the robot charging outdoors exposed to rain or direct midday sun. Spa & Pool Supplies recommends charging and storing your cleaner in a dry, ventilated shed or garage.

During Melbourne’s cooler winter months when pool usage drops, store the cordless robot with a partial battery charge (around 50 to 60 percent) rather than letting it sit depleted. Periodic top-up charges every two months maintain chemical stability in the lithium cells, ensuring your cleaner is immediately ready for action when warm spring swimming returns.

Eco-Friendly Swimming and Energy Savings with Cordless Cleaners

In an era of rising energy costs and environmental focus, cordless robotic pool cleaners offer Melbourne homeowners one of the greenest, most energy-efficient ways to maintain a pool. Unlike suction-style pool cleaners that require the main 1,000 to 1,500-watt pool pump to run for hours on end, cordless robots draw power exclusively from their internal lithium batteries, which recharge for pennies worth of off-peak electricity.

By decoupling your pool cleaning from the central filtration system, you can significantly scale back primary filtration pump hours or run energy-saving variable-speed pumps at ultra-low speeds. This reduction in primary pump usage saves Melbourne households hundreds of dollars annually on household power bills, allowing your cordless cleaner to offset its purchase price quickly.

Water conservation is another substantial benefit of cordless robotic cleaning. Traditional suction cleaners suck dirt directly into your pool sand or cartridge filter, leading to clogged media that requires frequent backwashing or high-pressure cartridge spraying. Because cordless robotic cleaners collect debris inside internal microfiltration baskets, they eliminate unnecessary backwashing cycles and conserve thousands of liters of treated pool water each year.

Additionally, removing organic contaminants before they dissolve reduces pool chemical consumption. Fallen leaves, insects, and fine windblown dust are locked away in the robot filter canister, preventing chloramine formation and stabilizing chlorine demand.
