To determine the ideal robot for your Melbourne pool, evaluate the following essential factors:

  • ✓Pool Interior Surface: Smooth fibreglass and vinyl pools need specialized foam brushes or wonder-brushes for grip, while concrete, pebblecrete, and tiled finishes require durable PVC combi-brushes.

Operational Guide and Routine Care for Pool Cleaning Robots

Operating a modern pool cleaning robot is remarkably simple, but following standard operational procedures ensures peak performance and prolonged component life. Spa & Pool Supplies recommends establishing a consistent routine each time you launch a cleaning cycle to keep your pool immaculate and preserve your investment.

Follow these standard operational steps to maximize machine efficiency and service life:

  1. 1Submerge and bleed trapped air: Carefully lower the cleaning robot into the pool by its sturdy handle, gently tilting it underwater until all air bubbles vent and the unit sinks smoothly to the pool floor.
  2. 2Position the control module: Place the digital power supply box at least 3.5 meters from the pool water's edge in a shaded, well-ventilated spot, plugged into a compliant, weatherproof outdoor RCD power point.
  3. 3Select and engage cleaning cycle: Connect the swivel cable securely to the power unit, switch on the system, and choose your desired cleaning profile (floor-only, rapid clean, or full floor, wall, and waterline mode).
  4. 4Retrieve and flush filter basket: At the end of the cleaning cycle, pull the floating cable gently until the robot is within reach, lift the unit out using the handle, empty the collected debris, and rinse the filter panels thoroughly with a garden hose.

Proper cable handling is critical for preventing kinks and long-term twisting. Never pull forcefully on the power cable to lift the water-filled robot from the pool; always bring the unit to the coping edge and lift strictly using the molded handle. Spa & Pool Supplies also advises laying the swivel cable flat along the lawn in the sun periodically to discharge any residual rotational tension.

Storage between cleaning cycles should always be in a shaded, sheltered space away from harsh sunlight and freezing temperatures. Storing your pool robot on an ergonomic caddy or inside a pool shed protects synthetic tracks, silicone seals, and electrical cables from premature degradation, ensuring trouble-free automated cleaning every season.

Eco-Friendly Energy Savings and Water Conservation

As electricity tariffs and utility costs rise across Victoria, adopting energy-efficient pool maintenance equipment has become a major priority for Melbourne households. Traditional suction cleaners operate by drawing suction from your main pool pump, which often requires running a 1,000 to 1,500-watt motor for six to eight hours every day. In contrast, an advanced robotic pool cleaner from Spa & Pool Supplies operates on a self-contained low-voltage motor consuming merely 100 to 180 watts per cycle, reducing cleaning electricity consumption by up to 80 percent.

These substantial energy savings allow a robotic pool cleaner to pay for itself within just a few swimming seasons. By relieving your main pool filtration pump of vacuuming duties, you can reduce overall daily pump run times or switch to an ultra-efficient variable-speed pump running at lower, whisper-quiet speeds, locking in permanent household energy reductions.

In addition to electrical efficiency, robotic pool cleaners offer critical water conservation benefits. Traditional cleaners deposit debris straight into sand or glass filtration systems, causing rapid pressure spikes that demand frequent backwashing. Backwashing dumps hundreds of liters of heated, chemically treated water directly into the stormwater drain. Because robotic cleaners trap debris internally in easy-rinse canisters, they eliminate needless backwashing and save thousands of liters of pool water annually.

Finally, independent debris capture reduces chemical demand and sanitiser consumption. By removing organic leaves, insects, and fine dust from the pool before they decay, robotic cleaners prevent the formation of chloramines and suppress algae growth.
